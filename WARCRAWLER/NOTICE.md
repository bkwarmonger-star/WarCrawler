# Acceptable use

warcrawler is a general-purpose crawling engine intended for **lawful** use:
security research and threat intelligence, academic and journalistic research,
data aggregation, and archiving.

The engine is content-neutral — it crawls whatever you point it at — and you are
responsible for how you use it. In particular:

- Crawl only systems and services you are **authorised** to access, and comply
  with applicable law, contracts, and terms of service in your jurisdiction.
- `respect_robots: false`, aggressive throttling, and stealth features exist for
  legitimate scenarios (your own infrastructure, authorised testing, research).
  Using them to disrupt services or evade access controls may be illegal.
- Do **not** use this tool to locate, collect, or distribute illegal material.
